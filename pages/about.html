Our Journey

From a Small Workshop to a National Brand

HM Carpets began with a simple dream — to preserve Pakistan's rich carpet weaving heritage while making premium quality accessible to every home. What started as a small family workshop in Gujranwala has grown into a trusted name across Pakistan.

Today, our state-of-the-art factory in More Eminabad and beautiful showroom in Civil Lines, Gujranwala stand as testimony to years of dedication, master craftsmanship, and unwavering customer trust.

Our Journey

Milestones & Achievements

2010

The Beginning

Started as a small family workshop in Gujranwala with a vision to revive traditional Pakistani carpet weaving.

2014

Factory Expansion

Established our modern manufacturing unit in More Eminabad to meet growing demand for premium quality carpets.

2017

Showroom Opening

Opened our flagship showroom at Civil Lines, Gujranwala — a premium retail destination for our customers.

2019

Nationwide Delivery

Launched free nationwide delivery service — bringing HM Carpets quality to every corner of Pakistan.

2022

Wholesale Network

Built a network of 200+ shop partners across 50+ cities — empowering small businesses nationwide.

2026

Digital Excellence

Launched our world-class digital presence with hmcarpets.pk — making premium carpets accessible to every Pakistani online.
